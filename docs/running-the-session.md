# Agency Zero — running the pre-build session

A one-pager for facilitating the live session with Roger + Sarah. The app is the
artefact; the room is the point. Your job is to get the strawman **argued with
and corrected**, not to present it.

> **The one rule:** run the whole session from **one browser on one device.** All
> edits save to that browser's local storage — they do **not** sync across
> machines or people. At the end, hit **Export** in the Workshop to save the JSON.
> That's the only backup.

---

## Before the room (5 min)

1. Open the app, log in with the shared passphrase.
2. Do a dry run of the flow below so nothing surprises you live.
3. Decide who "drives" (holds the laptop). One driver, one screen, projected.
4. Optional: hit **Reset** in the Workshop so you start from the clean strawman.

## The flow — follow the nav left to right

The screens are ordered to build trust first, then open up the argument, then
land the decision.

| # | Screen | What you do in the room | Time |
|---|--------|-------------------------|------|
| **1** | **Today** | Establish trust. "This is our real FY26, verified against the retainer book. We are healthy." Do **not** speculate here — just agree the starting point is true. | 5 min |
| **2** | **Confirm** | Walk the global assumptions once. Get a nod (or a correction) on each: owner comp, loaded cost, fee compression, the $1m floor. Edits here flow through every screen. | 10 min |
| **3** | **The Rebuild** | Set the frame: *if we founded Sunny today, who would we hire?* Build **from zero**, never subtract from today. This is the north-star, per stage. | 5 min |
| **4** | **Workshop** ⭐ | **The centre of gravity.** Go process by process. For each: *Is it required? Who does it today? How much can AI take? How time-heavy is it?* The headcount falls out of the answers — no hour estimates. Argue. Re-assign people. Cut what's dead. Autosaves as you go. | 40–60 min |
| **5** | **The Model** | Now show the P&L that fell out of the Workshop. Push the sliders live — growth, adoption, fee compression — to stress-test. **Save** the corrected scenario ("Run the model"). | 15 min |
| **6** | **Horizons** | The 18–24 month glide path. Attrition-led vs restructure-led. When does it break even. This is the "how", not the "whether". | 10 min |
| **7** | **Scenarios** | Land it. Status Quo vs Agency Zero vs Middle Path, side by side, plus whatever you saved. The Middle Path is the realistic target. | 10 min |

## Facilitation notes

- **Lead with growth, not cuts.** The capacity dividend is a *choice*: take it as
  profit (same revenue, fewer people) **or** as growth (same people, more revenue).
  Growth is the preferred framing — the subtraction is an *output*, never the pitch.
- **Everything is a strawman on purpose.** All process/automation seeds are marked
  `assumed`. They exist to be corrected in the room. "Tell me where this is wrong"
  is the whole game.
- **Keep it role-only.** No client names, no employee names — ever. Roles only.
- **The relationship layer stays human.** The phone, firefighting, entertainment,
  supplier/talent relationships, training — modelled explicitly as human. That's
  the strongest answer to "who would we hire."
- **Use the confidence tags.** Hover shows `verified` / `estimated` / `assumed`
  and tolerance bands. Outputs render as ranges — the honesty is the credibility.

## When you're done

1. **Workshop → Export.** Save the JSON somewhere durable. This is the corrected
   model; local storage is not a backup.
2. Confirm the saved scenario is on the **Scenarios** screen.
3. If the session needs to continue on another device, that device starts fresh —
   Export/hand-off is manual today (there's no import yet).

## What does NOT persist / gotchas

- Storage is **per-browser, per-device** local storage. No cloud, no database.
- Clearing site data or an incognito window = a blank slate.
- Two devices editing = two separate copies, no merge.
- Saved scenarios are capped at 30 (oldest drop off).
